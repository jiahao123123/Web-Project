**Project Description:**
This project dynamically captures the changes of a famous graffiti street called **Clarion Alley in San Francisco,** exploring the impact of time on urban street art. By using Google Maps' Street View feature to record the same location at different times and year, this work collects and compares street scenes at various moments. This series of street view screenshots not only showcases the life cycle of graffiti art but also documents the continuous evolution of the urban environment and the temporality of graffiti works. This project aims to capture the fleeting moments of art in the passage of time and the erase of graffiti, while also reflecting on the social significance of urban renewal and the use of public space.

**Artistic Idea:**
This project serves as a reflection on the relentless march of time and its imprint on urban cultural artifacts, particularly ephemeral street art. By highlighting changes over time, the artwork underscores the ephemeral nature of graffiti, marking its fleeting presence in the urban tapestry. The extended street perspective offered by this work is like a visual journey through time, envisioning a future while contemplating the transient and often invisible layers of urban life. This approach not only memorializes transient art forms but also delves into themes of urban change, social memory, and the invisible boundaries between the present and the future. The project challenges viewers to reconsider the notions of permanence and ephemerality, urging them to contemplate the urban space, the relentless flow of time, and the intricate relationship between art, memory, and the future. Through this lens, the project probes deeper into the significance of preserving street art within the fluctuating urban fabric, highlighting its value as a cultural and temporal dialogue.
